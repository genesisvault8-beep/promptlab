Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

PromptLab is built to work entirely in your browser. This page explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

What we don't do

  • We don't run a backend server for PromptLab. There's no database where your prompts, outputs, or API keys are stored.
  • We don't require an account, email, or sign-up of any kind.
  • We don't see, log, or transmit your OpenRouter API key to anywhere except OpenRouter itself.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't collect any to begin with.

How your API key is handled

When you paste your OpenRouter API key into PromptLab, it's stored in your browser's local storage only. Every request — prompt generation, comparison, style extraction — goes directly from your browser to OpenRouter's API. Genesis Vault's servers are never in that request path.

Your prompts and saved data

Prompts you save in the Prompt Library are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). If you clear your browser data or switch devices, that data doesn't transfer, because it was never sent to us in the first place.

Third-party services

PromptLab sends your requests to OpenRouter, which routes them to the AI model you select (Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, or others available on OpenRouter). Those requests are subject to OpenRouter's own privacy policy and the policies of the underlying model provider. We don't control or have visibility into that layer.

Analytics

If PromptLab uses any basic, privacy-respecting analytics (such as aggregate page-view counts) to understand traffic, it does not collect personal data, prompt content, or API keys. No cross-site tracking or ad-targeting cookies are used.

Cookies

PromptLab doesn't use cookies for tracking. Browser localStorage is used only to save your preferences and saved prompts locally on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect it. Continued use of PromptLab after changes means you accept the updated terms.
